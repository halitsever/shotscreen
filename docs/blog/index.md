# Mac screenshot guides

<p class="lead">Short, practical guides to taking screenshots on a Mac, checked against Apple's own documentation. Where a website is the subject, they also show how Shotscreen Browser, a free frameless browser for clean website screenshots, fits in.</p>

## [Mac screenshot shortcuts: every key combo](/blog/mac-screenshot-shortcuts)

Every Mac screenshot shortcut in one table: Shift-Command-3, 4 and 5, window captures, copying to the clipboard instead of a file, and how to change the keys.

## [Where do screenshots go on Mac?](/blog/where-do-screenshots-go-on-mac)

Mac screenshots are saved to the desktop by default. How to find old screenshots, change where new ones are saved, and why a screenshot sometimes is not there.

## [How to crop a screenshot on Mac](/blog/how-to-crop-a-screenshot-on-mac)

Crop a screenshot on Mac with the floating thumbnail, with Preview and Command-K, or in Photos. Plus how to skip cropping by capturing the right area first.

## [How to take a full page screenshot on Mac](/blog/full-page-screenshot-mac)

Take a full page screenshot on Mac in Safari, Chrome or Firefox with no extension, or in one step with a frameless browser, and what goes wrong on long pages.

## [Full page screenshot in Chrome without an extension](/blog/full-page-screenshot-chrome)

Chrome can take a full page screenshot with no extension: DevTools has Capture full size screenshot. Steps for Mac and Windows, phone sizes and pitfalls.

## [How to get a higher resolution screenshot on Mac](/blog/higher-resolution-screenshot-mac)

Why Mac screenshots look blurry, how Retina scaling sets their pixel size, and how to get a sharper, higher resolution screenshot of the screen or a web page.

## [How to print screen on a Mac](/blog/print-screen-on-mac)

There is no Print Screen key on a Mac. The shortcuts that replace it, what happened to Grab, mapping a key of your own, and how to print a screenshot on paper.

## [Can a website detect screenshots?](/blog/can-a-website-detect-screenshots)

A website cannot see a screenshot you take with your operating system. What a page can notice (keys, focus, DevTools, page changes) and what it cannot.
